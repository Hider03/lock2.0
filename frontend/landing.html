How QRKey Works in 3 Simple Steps

  1. Sign Up & Tag: Register your items in your secure dashboard. Download and attach the unique QR code to your item.

  2. Item is Found: A finder scans the QR code, which opens a simple public recovery page with your instructions.

  3. Instant Notification: The finder sends an anonymous message through the page, and you instantly receive an email alert with their contact details.
